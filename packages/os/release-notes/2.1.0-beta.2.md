Thank you for enrolling in the umbrelOS Beta Program.

umbrelOS 2.1 Beta 2 lets you find anything on your Umbrel by describing it, recognizes the people in your photos, brings new FailSafe modes for HDD NAS devices, and a lot more.

### What's new

- **Smart search:** Find anything on your Umbrel by describing it. Search photos and videos by what's in them, documents and scanned pages by the text inside them, and audio files by what's said in them.

- **People in Photos:** Photos now recognizes the faces in your photos and videos and groups them by person.

- **Automatic transcriptions:** Speech in your videos and audio files is now transcribed automatically in 39 languages, so you can read and search what was said.

- **Map view in Photos:** See where every photo was taken. The new map view places your photos and videos on a globe, so you can spin it and zoom into a place.

- **New FailSafe modes for HDD NAS devices:** Protect your hard drives against any one or any two drives failing. Storage setup and Storage Manager are redesigned too, showing your drives at a glance and recommending the best setup for them.

- **A faster, refined Files:** Files has a new experience that's faster and made for every screen, and lets you look inside ZIP archives without extracting them.

- New accessibility settings for text size and increased contrast.

- Support for more hardware, including devices with UFS storage, and booting Raspberry Pi 4 from a USB drive.

- Everything from 2.1 Beta 1, including documents in Files, Umbrel apps for Android and Windows, and bridged networking and USB devices in Machines.

- Several performance improvements, refinements, and bug fixes.
