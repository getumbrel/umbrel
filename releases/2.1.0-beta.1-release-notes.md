Thank you for enrolling in the umbrelOS Beta Program.

umbrelOS 2.1 Beta 1 opens your documents right in Files, brings Umbrel apps to Android and Windows, and a lot more.

### What's new

- **Documents in Files:** Open PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint decks, and spreadsheets in a new viewer, and make quick edits right where they are. Fill in, annotate, and sign PDFs, fix text, and edit cells and formulas.

- **Umbrel for Android and Windows:** The new [Umbrel app for Android](https://link.umbrel.com/android-app) backs up your photos and videos automatically, and the new [Umbrel app for Windows](https://link.umbrel.com/windows-app) adds your Umbrel as a drive in File Explorer. With the apps for [iPhone](https://link.umbrel.com/ios-app) and [Mac](https://link.umbrel.com/macos-app), there's now an Umbrel app for every device you own.

- **New in Machines:** Machines can now join your home network directly, so apps like Home Assistant OS can find your smart home devices. You can now also give a machine access to the USB devices plugged into your Umbrel, like Zigbee adapters, drives, and Coral accelerators, and copy and paste between a machine and your computer. There's also a new touch console for using Machines from your phone or tablet.

- Storage Manager and FailSafe support for drives behind SAS HBA cards.

- Updated Linux kernel and NVIDIA driver with support for newer hardware, including RTX 50-series GPUs.

- Several performance improvements, refinements, and bug fixes.
